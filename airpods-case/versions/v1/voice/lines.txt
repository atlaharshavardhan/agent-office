You just put your AirPods in your friend's case. Will they charge? First, how does charging even work?
Two tiny spring pins in each slot touch two gold dots on the stem. Magnets hold the bud down. Power flows from the case battery, and a chip in the earbud decides how fast to fill it.
Your friend's case has the same pins in the same place. So yes, they charge. But your iPhone notices.
It pops up, Mismatched AirPods, because your buds and case were set up as a pair.
Put them back in their own case, or set them up again after your friend removes them from Find My.
A different generation can fit perfectly, and still not work properly.
