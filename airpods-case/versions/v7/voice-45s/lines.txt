Can your AirPods charge in someone else's case? Yes. But there's a catch.
The secret is hidden inside this tiny case.
Magnets guide the earbuds into position, lining up their tiny charging contacts.
Once those metal contacts connect, the case delivers power directly to the earbuds.
Power travels from the case's battery, through its charging circuit, into each AirPod.
But charging is only part of the story. Your iPhone may notice the AirPods and case don't belong together.
A compatible case may charge your AirPods, but different models aren't always interchangeable.
So yes, another compatible case can charge your AirPods. But not every case will work.
