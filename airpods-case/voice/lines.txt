Your AirPods can charge inside your friend's case. But there's a catch.
Inside every charging case is a tiny power system.
When the earbuds snap into place, their metal contacts connect to the charging circuit.
Power flows from the case's battery, through its circuit board, and into the earbuds.
But charging doesn't necessarily mean everything will work normally.
A compatible case can charge your AirPods. But incompatible models may not fit or charge correctly.
So yes. A different compatible case can charge your AirPods.
