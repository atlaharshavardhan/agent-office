Can your AirPods charge inside someone else's case? Yes. But there's a catch.
The secret is hidden inside this tiny charging case.
Magnets guide the earbuds into the correct position, lining up their tiny charging contacts.
Once those metal contacts connect, the case can deliver power directly to the earbuds.
Power travels from the case's battery, through its charging circuit, and into each AirPod.
But charging is only part of the story. Your iPhone may recognize that the AirPods or case don't belong together.
A compatible case may charge your AirPods, but different models aren't always interchangeable.
So yes, another compatible case can charge your AirPods. Just don't assume every AirPods case will work.
