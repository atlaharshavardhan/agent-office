# InvisiTech Channel Guide

Every agent reads this file before it works. Harsha (the director) and the
Feedback Learner are the only ones who may change it, and only after the
channel owner approves the change.

## Who we are
- **InvisiTech** is a **faceless** YouTube channel about the invisible technology
  behind everyday life (OTPs, FASTag, UPI, Wi-Fi, maps, cards, ...).
- Narration is in **English**. Hindi/Telugu versions may come later.
- Viewers are **non-tech people**. If a 14-year-old or a grandparent can't follow
  it, it is too complex.

## Format: "follow the thing"
- Every video follows **one thing on its journey** (a code, a payment, a signal,
  a packet) from start to finish. The viewer rides along with it.
- Each scene = one stop on the journey. Say where we are, what happens here,
  and why it matters, then move on.
- Use everyday comparisons (post office, relay race, security guard), never jargon
  without a one-line plain explanation.

## Structure (checked by the Rule Validator)
1. **Hook** – the very first line is a *surprising, true fact*, spoken within the
   first **10 seconds** (about 25 words). No "Hi guys", no channel intro.
2. **Main** – the journey, step by step. Every scene must move the story forward.
3. **Outro** – land the journey, one takeaway the viewer will remember, and a
   short, natural call to action. No long goodbye.
- **Long videos are 4–8 minutes**, chosen by how much the topic needs. Never
  dragged: no repeating the same point, no filler ("basically", "as I said",
  "without further ado"), no padding to hit a length.

## Look
- **Premium dark look**: deep navy / near-black backgrounds, clean light text,
  one or two accent colours per video.
- **Original flat cartoon style**. Simple shapes, clear silhouettes, readable at
  phone size. Never copy another channel's or brand's characters or art.
- **Soft shadows instead of glow effects.** No neon glow, no lens flares.
- **No mascot.** The "thing" we follow (the OTP, the tag, the payment) can be drawn
  as an object, but we have no recurring channel character.
- Long videos are **16:9 (1920×1080)**. Shorts are 9:16 (1080×1920). Never stretch
  or squash an image to fit; re-frame it instead.

## Facts, policy and copyright
- Every number and claim must be true and checkable. If unsure, say less.
- Follow YouTube policy: no misleading claims, no scams, no instructions that help
  fraud (we explain how OTP fraud works only to help viewers stay safe).
- Use only original visuals and text. Name brands only when needed to explain
  (e.g. NPCI runs FASTag's network); never use their logos as decoration.

## Money
- Nothing that costs money or credits runs without the owner's approval
  (vidIQ research is allowed freely; Higgsfield is never used unless the owner says so).
