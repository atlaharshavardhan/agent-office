## Verified facts (WHOOP 5.0 / MG, May 2025)
- ~34.7 x 24 x 10.6 mm, ~26.5 g, IP68 to 10 m, ~14 days battery (official)
- Optical sensor samples 26x per second (official)
- Ambiq Micro Cortex-M4 SoC with BLE 5.0 LE; Micron NAND flash (TechInsights teardown summary)
- ECG via clasp on MG only (official)
- Unverified: 5 LEDs (3 green, 1 red, 1 IR) + 4 photodiodes; silicon-anode battery -> mark "Illustrative"
- No free 3D model, no iFixit teardown -> build pod in code; board layout illustrative
- Rule 14: no real brand logos on the pod
