# AC video: narration v3, "why first, then build it" (for Harsh's OK)

Rule for every part: first say **why we need it** (the problem), then **introduce it** (the fix), with a "let's build our own AC" voice. On-screen labels stay the same.

## Fact check
- Heat flows only from hot to cold: correct, which is why the compressor heats the gas above outdoor temperature.
- Compressing a gas heats it; the pressure drop through the expansion valve cools the liquid: correct.
- The evaporator coil condenses moisture from the air (dehumidifies), and it drains outside: correct.
- The thermostat cycles the compressor off at the set temperature: correct (for a basic on/off AC; inverter ACs slow down instead of fully stopping, which is OK to leave out here).
- Dust on the coil reduces airflow and cooling, and the filter protects the coil: correct.
