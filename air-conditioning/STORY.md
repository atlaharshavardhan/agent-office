# How You'd Invent Air Conditioning: story (3D window version)

- **Format:** 1080×1920, 30 fps, exactly 1350 frames (45 s). The story runs 0–43 s (frames 0–1289); the end card runs 43–45 s (frames 1290–1349).
- **Look:** a 3D room in Blender, with the same straight-on camera on the same window, plus Remotion overlays.
- **Overlays:**
  - a big outlined temperature at the top
  - one big word per beat
  - heat squiggles and arrows
  - timed captions
- **AC:** there's no wall-mounted split AC and no separate outdoor unit anywhere. The only AC is one box in the window.
