## Fact check
- 8 bits = 1 letter (ASCII); "Hi" = 2 × 8 = 16 bits → 16 s at one blink a second. ✔
- A gigabit wire carries 1,000,000,000 bits a second. A book (about 1 MB = 8 million bits) takes about 8 ms; a human blink takes 100–400 ms. ✔
- Connecting every pair of 10 houses: 10 × 9 ÷ 2 = 45 wires. ✔ (The reference showed "40" on screen; we show 45.)
- IP address identifies each device; the router reads it and forwards. ✔
- Data is split into packets, each carrying source and destination addresses. ✔
- Fibre: light by total internal reflection in glass. ✔
- 150–200 cable faults a year, 70–80% from fishing and anchors (ICPC); 570–600 cable systems in service (TeleGeography). ✔
- Never share an OTP: banks and RBI say no bank will ever ask for it. ✔
