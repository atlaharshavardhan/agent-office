Your music just stopped. So how did your AirPods know you took one out?
Inside, there's a tiny skin-detect sensor, right where the bud touches your ear. An infrared light, with a light sensor beside it.
When it's in your ear, it shines invisible light into your skin, and reads what bounces back. Your skin sends it back in its own way.
A table, or a pocket, doesn't match that pattern. Older AirPods used simpler sensors that a pocket could fool. This one can tell skin apart.
The moment it stops seeing skin, the H2 chip knows, and tells your phone over Bluetooth to pause.
Put it back in, it sees skin again, and your music plays on.
