`scripts/convert_assets.py` exports Three.js geometry only from a repository that has a permissive licence. Neither repository qualifies at the moment.

## What is in the scene

All 3D geometry, materials, lighting and animation are **original** and generated by the Python scripts in `scripts/`:

- The accelerator package (two compute dies, 8 HBM stacks, interposer, substrate, stiffener ring, capacitors, BGA, metal fragments) follows the publicly described layout of a Blackwell-class package. The sizes are illustrative.
- The baseboard (copper traces with pulse attributes, vias, VRM inductors, MLCCs, gold contact rows, connectors) is original procedural geometry.

## Brand

The words "NVIDIA" are etched on the stiffener ring and printed on the board. Harsh chose to show the NVIDIA brand for this video (an exception to InvisiTech rule 14, 2026-11-16).

- The NVIDIA "eye" logo artwork is **not** reproduced. Only the word is set, in the Inter font.
- The back of the ring reads "AI ACCELERATOR · 3D ILLUSTRATION".
- In the edit, keep the on-screen line **"3D illustration · not affiliated with NVIDIA"**.

NVIDIA is a trademark of NVIDIA Corporation.
