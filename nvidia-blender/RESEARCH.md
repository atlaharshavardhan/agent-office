## 7. Paper value, not cash

- Market cap is shares × price, so the $2T is a drop in what investors will pay for the stock. **NVIDIA did not spend or lose $2T in cash.**
- The business was still growing at its last report:
  - Q2 FY2027 revenue was $96.2B (+106% YoY), and gross margin was 75%.
  - Q3 guidance is **$108B ±2%** (excluding China data-center compute).
  - In August, Kress guided about 70% growth for FY2028.
  - Free cash flow was **$21.3B** in the quarter.
  - **Cash, cash equivalents and marketable debt securities totaled $56.6B** at quarter-end. That excludes about $42.8B of equity stakes.
  - Nvidia returned about $26B to shareholders in the quarter.
  - Sources:
    - https://quartr.com/events/nvidia-corporation-nvda-q2-2027_33dRoFp6 (Aug 2026)
