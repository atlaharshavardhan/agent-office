## Key nuance for the script
- **Ship vs rent.** Shipping these chips *into China* needs a US licence. Renting their compute *from Southeast Asia* to Chinese firms was a legal grey zone, and the US moved to close it on Nov 16, 2026. The investigations concern whether hardware was actually **diverted**, or whether false declarations were made (Singapore). **No charges have been filed and no diversion has been proven.** Bloomberg found no evidence of diversion, and NVIDIA and Megaspeed deny it.
- Avoid "by the hour" and "smuggled". Use "rents computing power" and "investigators are probing whether…".

## Suggested script lines (≤ 25 words total)
1. "Bloomberg estimates Megaspeed imported at least $4.6 billion of NVIDIA hardware." (12 words)
2. "Renting that power to Chinese firms abroad wasn't banned. Shipping chips into China was." (13 words)

Alternative line 2 if space allows a date: "Renting it to Chinese firms from abroad was a loophole, until a November rule."
