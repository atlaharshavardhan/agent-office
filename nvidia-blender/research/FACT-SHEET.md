## Verdict on the $2T figure

- **Is "$2 trillion" accurate?** Almost. It refers to the **current (Oct–Nov 2026) slide**, not the DeepSeek episode. None of the earlier drawdowns comes close to $2T. The largest one, Jan–Apr 2025, was about $1.3–1.4T.
- **Peak:** record close on **Oct 6, 2026** at $239.24 per share, a market cap of about **$5.77–5.78 trillion**. Sources: Businessday NG (citing Dow Jones Market Data), Motley Fool, Time.
- **Last close found:** **Nov 13, 2026**, about **$3.92 trillion**. This was the first close below $4T since July 2025 (CNBC, Businessday).
- **Loss from close to close:** about **$1.85 trillion, roughly 32%**. CNBC says "roughly $1.7 trillion". Businessday and Yahoo/Euronews say "more than $1.8 trillion". A Yahoo Finance headline says "nearly $2tn".
- **Intraday today (Nov 16, 2026):** quotes of $155.70–$159 per share put the market cap at about **$3.75–3.83T**. That is **about $1.95–2.03T below the peak**, so the $2T line was only crossed intraday today. This is NOT a confirmed close (marked UNVERIFIED as a closing figure).
- **Safe wording:**
  - **"Nearly $2 trillion"**, or **"about $1.8 trillion in five weeks"**, measured from the Oct 6 record close to the Nov 13 close.
  - A flat "$2 trillion" is only defensible with a caveat such as "at one point" or "intraday", and only if the Nov 16 close (or later) confirms it.
- **Title suggestion:** "How NVIDIA Lost Nearly $2 Trillion".
- **Nothing has recovered yet.** The next catalyst is the Q3 FY2027 earnings report on **Nov 17, 2026**.
- **Do not conflate episodes.** The $589B DeepSeek day (Jan 27, 2025) is the record for a single day. The $2T figure is a cumulative five-to-six-week drawdown in 2026.

## Details per episode

### 1. Summer 2024
- Nvidia's market cap reached $3.34 trillion on June 18, 2024, passing Microsoft to become the world's most valuable company. Source: https://fortune.com/2024/06/21/nvidia-market-cap-world-most-valuable-company-nvda-stock-selloff/ (2024-06-21)
- A three-day slide erased about $430B and put the stock in correction territory. Bloomberg data called it the largest three-day value loss for any company at the time. Source: https://www.business-standard.com/world-news/nvidia-hits-correction-territory-as-slump-wipes-out-430-bn-in-market-value-124062500129_1.html (2024-06-25)
- The market value fell about $900B, down about 27% from the June highs, within two months. Source: https://techovedas.com/nvidia-900-billion-fall-whats-behind-the-chipmakers-market-tumble/ (Aug 2024; low-tier source, so the exact figure is UNVERIFIED)
- By Sep 4, 2024 the stock was still up 118% for the year, at a market value of about $2.7T. Source: https://daytraders.com/news/2024/09/04/nvidia-is-in-trouble (2024-09-04, syndicating CNN)

### 2. DeepSeek day: Jan 27, 2025
- Shares fell 17%, the biggest drop since March 2020, erasing **$589 billion**. That was the largest one-day loss of market value in US market history. Source: https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-01-27/asml-sinks-as-china-ai-startup-triggers-panic-in-tech-stocks (2025-01-27)
- Reuters-based coverage gave $593B. The previous record was about $279B, set by Nvidia in September 2024. Sources: https://www.business-standard.com/amp/markets/news/deepseek-sparks-ai-stock-selloff-nvidia-loses-record-593-bn-in-mcap-125012800095_1.html (2025-01-28) and https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/nvidia-loses-usd589-billion-in-market-cap-broad-stock-plunge-triggered-by-deepseek-ai-release (2025-01-27)
- Cause cited: fear that a cheap Chinese model would cut demand for expensive Nvidia chips. Source: https://www.nbcnews.com/business/business-news/nvidia-loses-market-value-chinese-ai-startup-deepseek-debut-rcna189431 (2025-01-27)

### 3. Jan–Apr 2025 drawdown
- The stock fell 32% from its January peak, taking the market cap from $3.66T to $2.48T (mid-April). Source: https://www.fxempire.com/forecasts/article/the-trump-trade-how-tariffs-and-ai-demand-fears-are-shaping-nvidias-future-1514668 (Apr 2025)
- The year-to-date closing low came on Apr 4, 2025 at $94.31, a market cap of about $2.3T. Source: https://benzinga.com/z/45524565 (May 2025). CNBC also calls Apr 4 the 52-week low: https://www.cnbc.com/2025/07/15/analysts-raise-nvidia-price-targets-after-us-decision-on-china-sales.html (2025-07-15)
- The stock lost $393B over Apr 3–4, 2025 after the tariff announcement. Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2025/04/04/technology-stocks-fall-for-a-second-session-led-by-tesla-and-nvidia.html (2025-04-04)
- It had lost more than $1.3T, down 36%, by Apr 21, 2025. Source: https://finviz.com/news/27854/nvidia-has-now-lost-more-than-13-trillion-in-market-cap-is-it-finally-time-to-buy-the-stock (Motley Fool syndication, 2025-04-21)
- Recovery: about $1T regained by May 2025 (https://benzinga.com/z/45524565). Nvidia became the first company valued at $5T on Oct 29, 2025, closing at $207.04, about $5.03T. Source: https://globalnews.ca/news/11501362/nvidia-ai-5-trillion-worth (2025-10-29); also https://fortune.com/2025/10/29/nvidia-first-5-trillion-company-ceo-jensen-huang-500-billion-revenue-blackwell-rubin-gpus-china/ (2025-10-29)

### 4. Oct–Nov 2026 (the "$2 trillion" story)
- **Peak:** record close of $239.24 on Oct 6, 2026, a market cap of about $5.78T. The intraday high was $243.37, and $6T would have required about $249 per share. Sources: https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/11/10/nvidia-stock-has-wiped-out-usd1-7-trillion-in-value-since-october-what-could-happen-if-jensen-huang-steps-down/ (2026-11-10) and https://cryptobriefing.com/nvidia-approaches-6-trillion-market-cap-as-stock-hits-record-high/ (Oct 2026)
- **Nov 5, 2026:** shares fell 9.26% to $181.60, about $450B lost. It was the worst day since DeepSeek. Source: https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/11/06/nvidia-just-had-its-worst-day-since-deepseek-losing-about-450-billion/ (2026-11-06)
- **Midterms:** CNBC reports that much of the decline came after the midterms, which brought sweeping Democratic wins and the passage of dozens of ballot measures that could slow the AI buildout. Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/nvidia-stock-jensen-affair-ai.html (2026-11-13)
- **Board probe:** on Nov 8 the board opened an independent investigation after reports linking Huang to a Chinese intelligence operation. Huang admitted an affair and denied compromising security. China rejected the spy allegations. Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/nvidia-jensen-huang-ceo-live-updates.html (2026-11-12)
- **CEO exit:** Huang left as CEO on Nov 12, 2026, and CFO Colette Kress became interim CEO. Sources disagree on whether he was ousted or resigned. Shares fell as much as 14% intraday, erasing as much as $601B, before closing down 4.3%. Sources: https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-12/nvidia-market-value-sinks-below-4-trillion-after-huang-resigns (2026-11-12) and https://www.nbcnews.com/business/markets/nvidia-stock-huang-resigns-rcna608958 (2026-11-12)
- **Below $4T:** Nvidia closed below a $4T market cap for the first time since July 2025. Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/nvidia-nvda-stock-jensen-huang-transition.html (2026-11-13)
- **Week of Nov 9–13:** the stock fell 13%, the steepest weekly drop since April 2025. It was the sixth straight losing week, the longest streak since 2012, with about $1.7T lost since early October. Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/nvidia-stock-jensen-affair-ai.html (2026-11-13)
- **Size of the loss:** $573B lost in that week, the largest weekly loss on record (Dow Jones Market Data), and more than $1.8T lost from the Oct 6 record of about $5.77T to about $3.92T. Source: https://businessday.ng/companies/article/nvidia-loses-1-8tn-in-market-value-as-shares-fall-below-4trn/ (2026-11-13/14)
- **"Nearly $2tn" headline:** Yahoo Finance/Euronews: "Nvidia has lost nearly $2tn in value". Source: https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/nvidia-lost-nearly-2tn-value-135308595.html (Nov 2026; exact date UNVERIFIED)
- **Today, Nov 16, 2026:** quotes about $155.70–$159.12, a market cap of about $3.75–3.83T. The stock is down about 27% over one month. Sources: https://www.cnbc.com/quotes/NVDA and https://www.google.com/finance/quote/NVDA:NASDAQ (2026-11-16, intraday). The closing value is UNVERIFIED.
- **Board stance:** per a CNBC source, the board is not considering bringing Huang back, despite a "Stand with Jensen" petition with about 180K signatures. Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang.html (2026-11-16)
- **Earnings due Nov 17, 2026:** Nvidia guided Q3 FY27 revenue to $108B ±2%, and consensus is about $109.1B. Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/nvidia-earnings-jensen-huang-resignation.html (2026-11-16)

## Nvidia's rise in the AI boom
- Fiscal 2025 (year to Jan 2025): Data Center revenue was **$115.2B, up 142%**, and total revenue was $130.5B, up 114%. Source: https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-financial-results-for-fourth-quarter-and-fiscal-2025 (2025-02-26)
- Fiscal 2026: total revenue was **$215.9B, up 65%**. Q4 FY26 revenue was $68.1B, and Data Center revenue was $62.3B, up 75% year over year. Source: https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-2026/default.aspx (Feb 2026)
- Q2 FY2027 (reported Aug 26, 2026): revenue was **$96.2B, up 106%**. Data Center revenue was **$89.0B, up 117%**, and gross margin was 75%. Source: https://www.outlookbusiness.com/corporate/nvidia-quarter-results-revenue-profits-data-centre-chips-jensen-huang-ai-investment-risk (2026-08-27). The investor-relations primary page was not retrieved.
- Market share: IDC puts Nvidia at about **81%** of the AI data-center chip market for 2026. Silicon Analysts estimates about 80% by revenue, with a peak near 87% in 2024. In China, Nvidia's share fell to 34% in H1 2026 against Huawei's 28% (IDC data via Reuters). Source: https://siliconanalysts.com/analysis/amd-vs-nvidia-ai-gpu-market-share-2026 (2026). The IDC original is UNVERIFIED, and estimates range from 70% to 90%.
- Milestones: first $4T company in July 2025, then first $5T company on Oct 29, 2025. Source: https://fortune.com/2025/10/29/nvidia-first-5-trillion-company-ceo-jensen-huang-500-billion-revenue-blackwell-rubin-gpus-china/ (2025-10-29)

## Why investors panicked (2026)
1. **Regulation risk after the midterms.** The Democratic sweep and ballot measures against data centers raised fears of a slower AI buildout. Source: CNBC, https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/nvidia-stock-jensen-affair-ai.html (2026-11-13)
2. **Leadership shock.** Huang co-founded the company, and his exit came amid a scandal and a national-security probe. HSBC and Bernstein downgraded the stock, and Baird cut it to Neutral with a target of $200, down from $500. Sources: https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-12/nvidia-market-value-sinks-below-4-trillion-after-huang-resigns (2026-11-12) and https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/11/12/nvidia-is-bouncing-after-a-430-billion-wipeout-one-analyst-just-downgraded-it-wheres-the-price-target/ (2026-11-12)
3. **Circular financing.**
   - Bank of America estimated that, beyond a $500B financing platform, Nvidia had about $70B in equity stakes in AI companies and about $230B in guarantees as of August 2026.
   - Mizuho traders said the August financing announcement did not "do much to quiet the growing concerns around circular financing".
   - Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/11/nvidia-tries-to-quiet-circular-financing-accusations-wall-street-unsure-it-will-.html (2026-08-11)
